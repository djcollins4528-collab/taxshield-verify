## Tax and operational decisions prepared

An individually owned SMLLC generally defaults to disregarded-entity federal income
tax treatment absent an election. No S-corporation election or payroll setup is
made here. Federal income-reporting W-9 rules distinguish the owner's TIN from the
LLC EIN; owner enters sensitive identifiers privately with IRS/bank/authorized
counterparties, never in chat or Git.

Virginia's services rule supports a preliminary analysis of the scoped advisory
engagement as a service, subject to actual facts and any mixed/tangible elements.
It is not a nationwide exemption or a specific ruling. Determine customer-state
obligations before accepting out-of-state work. Estimated-tax/withholding decisions
require actual owner figures; no tax amount or automatic reserve percentage is set.
Contractual refund reserves do not by themselves determine tax recognition.

Current FinCEN guidance exempts US-created domestic entities from BOI reporting;
no BOI filing service is proposed. Bank ownership/KYC requests are a separate matter.

The private cashbook was exercised with synthetic receipts, refunds, fees and owner
funding, then cleared. Totals and reserve movements recalculated without formula
errors. Zero values describe the blank ledger, not verified financial balances.
Monthly procedure: match gross processor/bank receipts, record fees/refunds
separately, categorize owner funding/draws/transfers, attach receipts, reconcile
statements and review unpaid invoices/reserve obligations. No paid software added.
