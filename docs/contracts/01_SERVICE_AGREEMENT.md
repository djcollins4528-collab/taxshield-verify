## 2. Service standard and responsibilities

Supplier will use reasonable professional skill and care, the agreed methodology,
and a separately accountable reviewer of the exact report version. Customer supplies
accurate scope inputs, approved read-only access, available records and timely admin
assistance, and performs all changes. Conclusions concern the named controls and
recorded observation times. Unavailable evidence produces disclosed inconclusive
results; it is not replaced by assurances or invented events. No administrator
credential sharing or departed-user sign-in is permitted.

No promise of universal access removal, absence of compromise, future security,
compliance certification, SOC/CPA attestation or a favorable result is made. Customer
retains decisions about remediation, employment and business risk. These boundaries
do not excuse negligent performance or override mandatory rights.

## 3. Price, payment, changes and taxes

The pilot fee is $1,950 USD, one-time. $975 is due immediately before kickoff only
after successful preflight and signed authority; $975 is invoiced on secure delivery

hourly overage or collection of card credentials by Supplier is authorized. Payment

access revocation or an otherwise owed refund. Added scope needs a fixed-price written
change order signed by both authorized representatives before added work/charges.

## 4. Delivery, acceptance and remedies

The SOW defines objective completion and due dates. Customer has five business days
after delivery to identify factual/scope defects and explicitly accept; silence is
not acceptance. An unfavorable or inconclusive finding is not a defect. Supplier
corrects valid provider errors without added fees, normally within two business days
after sufficient information. One consolidated customer factual-correction round is
included; provider errors remain correctable without an artificial one-round cap.
A defect that prevents complete initial delivery is treated as provider inability
for refund purposes. Billing cannot be triggered by a knowingly incomplete draft.

Notify and pause for documented customer dependencies. After five business days
without resolution, offer a revised schedule or cancellation. Provider-caused delay
is not relabeled as a customer pause. For a provider-caused missed initial deadline,
Customer may choose a revised schedule or cancellation/full refund before complete
initial delivery. Third-party interruptions are promptly reported; they do not erase
the agreed cancellation/refund remedies or extend time silently.

## 5. Cancellation, termination and earned fees

Either party may terminate on written notice. Before kickoff, all collected fees
are refunded. For customer termination after kickoff, only fully completed documented
milestones are earned: collection $600; assessment/independent review $850; complete
initial report/debrief availability $300; retest delivered or request window expired
$200. Partial milestones are not charged. Collected money above earned fees is refunded
within ten business days; any earned shortfall alone is invoiced, due in seven calendar
days, without a termination penalty. Cancelling an unused retest before its request
window expires refunds its $200 allocation, which is not an additional charge.

Provider cancellation or inability before complete initial-report delivery requires
a full refund. Provider inability to deliver a timely requested included retest requires
a $200 refund. Supplier does not convert its own inability into customer cancellation.
These allocations/remedies govern termination for breach too; they do not waive
non-waivable remedies. Stop collection and revoke access on termination; return and
delete data under the DPA regardless of a payment dispute. Record actual settlement.

Confidential information includes non-public business information, security findings,
customer evidence, access arrangements, pricing negotiations and designated confidential
material. Use it only to perform/administer the agreement; disclose only to approved
people/providers with need-to-know access and written confidentiality obligations.
Protect it with reasonable safeguards at least as protective as those used for one's
own comparable information. The receiving party must substantiate any exception for
information lawfully public, independently developed, already lawfully held or lawfully
received from another source without restriction. Compelled disclosure is limited to
what law requires; give prior notice and reasonable cooperation where lawful.

Confidentiality survives three years after termination; trade secrets while legally
protected, and personal data/access secrets for as long as retained. No publicity,
logo, case study, training dataset or testimonial permission is implied.

## 7. Data, report rights and subcontracting

templates, software and general know-how, excluding identifiable customer information.

mandatory return/subject rights are never conditioned on payment. Identified factual

No reseller, insurer, regulator or other third party receives a reliance right solely

Approved independent reviewers and subprocessors must meet the DPA; Supplier remains
responsible for subcontracted contractual performance. No transfer of this engagement
to another supplier without Customer's written consent, except as mandatory law allows.

engagement is limited to its $1,950 service fee. For breach of confidentiality, data

Caps and damage exclusions do not limit fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence,

included; each party remains responsible for its own acts under applicable law.
Counsel must assess caps, exclusions, third-party claims and insurer compatibility

The agreed correction/refund remedies are not stated as the exclusive remedy for
