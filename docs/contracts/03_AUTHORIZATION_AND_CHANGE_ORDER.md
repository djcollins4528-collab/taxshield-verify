# Customer authority, collection permission and change order

DRAFT v0.1 / UNSIGNED | November 5, 2026

## A. Customer identity and authority verification

Record privately: engagement ID, customer legal entity, signer's name/title/company
contact, source of authority, date verified, verifier and corroborating record.
Confirm company contact through an independently known company channel or existing
verified relationship, not solely the signer's newly supplied email. Verify ownership
or lawful delegated control of the tenant using an authorized admin and tenant ID.
An MSP needs both its documented delegation and authorization from the customer.
Do not collect personal identity documents when less intrusive verification suffices.
Resolve mismatched domains, signers or tenant IDs before consent/access/payment.

## B. Written authorization for the named engagement

Customer authorizes the named Supplier collector and reviewer, during the start/end
window recorded below, to process only the SOW's tenant, member identity, named static/
direct relationships and time-bounded records for the offboarding evidence review.
Customer confirms a lawful basis and necessary employee/third-party notices and
permissions; this is organizational permission, not purported employee consent.
Supplier will raise doubtful instructions and may suspend unauthorized processing.

Permitted operations: scoped read-only Graph/Entra requests, verification of record
coverage/pagination, minimized evidence capture, integrity references, assessment,
independent review, agreed secure report delivery and one newly authorized same-scope
retest. Customer performs sign-in disabling, session revocation and all relationship
changes. Authorizing observation does not authorize those write actions by Supplier.

Prohibited: write/admin mutation scopes, credential sharing, signing in as the departed
employee, unrestricted directory export, mailbox/message/file contents, unrestricted
sign-in histories, application-local session testing, scanning/exploitation, personnel
investigation, incident response, tenant-wide conclusions, or other tenants/identities.
Do not reuse access for research, AI prompts/training, prospecting or unrelated work.
Mandatory-source permission failure stops the fixed package before paid kickoff.

Private access schedule: tenant ID; target object ID; agreed relationship IDs/types;
Graph/API endpoint and exact permission for each; selected fields/date filters;
permission rationale; admin consent record; approved collector identity/application;
auth method without secret values; approval owner; start time; automatic expiry;
revocation owner; storage/delivery references; named reviewer and recipients.
Do not request broad permissions by default. Record actual admin-consent evidence.

Customer may revoke access immediately through its administrator and verified Supplier
contact. Supplier stops new collection upon revocation or termination, cancels queued
collection, closes sessions and coordinates removal of consents, grants and temporary
accounts. Collectible retention records follow the DPA; access revocation alone is
not evidence deletion. Confirm cleanup with timestamps and admin evidence rather than
promising control of customer systems. Retest requires a fresh authorized window.

Customer signature/name/title/date: UNSIGNED. Supplier acknowledgment/name/title/date:
UNSIGNED. Customer admin acknowledgment/date and actual access expiry: UNSET.

## C. Delivery and acceptance record

Authorized recipients and roles, verified recipient channel, report version/hash,
evidence-register version, delivery time/access expiry, debrief slots and factual
correction deadline: UNSET. Customer choice: ACCEPT / SPECIFIC DEFECTS / PENDING.
Record actual written response, defects and resolution. Silence remains PENDING.
No public links or automatic raw-evidence distribution. Identity of a recipient must
be verified before granting access; revoked or forwarded links confer no authority.

## D. Change order — not a permission bypass

Change ID and base engagement/version: UNSET. Requested by / authority verified: UNSET.
Describe exact additions/removals, evidence/source/permission changes, reason, exclusions,
new fixed fee/taxes, schedule, retention/subprocessor/insurance impact and dependencies.
State whether original obligations remain achievable. No automatic overage or implied
approval from a support conversation. If a change would make the seven-day initial
window impossible or require excluded identities/incident response, decline this pilot
and consider a separately reviewed service; do not relabel it as the same package.

Supplier and Customer sign/date: UNSIGNED. Original scope remains until both approve.
Retain both versions and authority records; never overwrite prior evidence or decisions.
