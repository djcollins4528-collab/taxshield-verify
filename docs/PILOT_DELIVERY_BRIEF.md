# Proofline scoped design-partner pilot — working draft

Commercial planning material only. No engagement is accepted by this document.
Intake and payment stay disabled until release authorization. No attorney approval
or insurance coverage is represented. Supporting legal terms require separate review.

## Offer hypothesis
An Independent Verification Review at approximately $1,500 for one agreed outcome,
one authorized organization, and one bounded evidence window. Validate scope and price
with the buyer before quoting. Continuous Assurance at approximately $1,250/month
is a later hypothesis, not an automatically renewing commitment.

Initial candidate outcome: verify that a named test identity's access was removed
across the explicitly approved Microsoft Entra/M365 evidence sources. Directory state
alone does not prove every application session or entitlement was revoked.

## Scope worksheet
- Customer organization and authorized approver: to be supplied.
- Business objective and exact claim to verify: to be supplied.
- Tenant identifier and evidence sources: to be approved before access.
- In-scope identities, applications, dates and exclusions: explicit bounded list.
- Read-only permission inventory and credential custodian: reviewed before collection.
- Data classification, retention period and deletion approver: agreed before submission.
- Collector, independent reviewer and delivery recipient: distinct approved roles.
- Deliverables, due date, price and reverification window: agreed in signed scope.

## Delivery sequence
1. Confirm authority, scope, exclusions, permissions and data-handling terms.
2. Verify approved billing workflow without provisioning from an unverified payment event.
3. Collect only approved evidence; record provenance, time, integrity hash and limitations.
4. Evaluate the claim as PASS, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE, NOT TESTED or NOT APPLICABLE.
5. Separate observed facts, inferred risks and unavailable evidence.
6. Have an independent reviewer approve the findings and report version.
7. Deliver through authenticated, tenant-scoped access; record delivery audit evidence.
8. Record remediation and new evidence; reverify without erasing the original finding.
9. Revoke temporary access, apply approved retention and verify custody records.

Acceptance requires all steps to pass in a synthetic rehearsal before real delivery.
Current staging only exercises a locked draft-review subset of this sequence.

## Report checklist
Executive summary; scope/authorization; collection dates; methodology; evidence inventory;
claim-to-evidence references; verification results; severity and business impact;
remediation; untested systems; confidence/limitations; reviewer determination;
version/date; integrity references; reverification outcome.
Synthetic examples must carry SAMPLE / SYNTHETIC on every deliverable.

## Support and incident routing to finalize
Assign an accountable operator, support address, response hours and escalation contact.
Before launch, rehearse access revocation, artifact withdrawal, evidence preservation,
scope assessment, recovery and stakeholder notification under the approved incident plan.
No support SLA, breach-notification deadline or operating approval is assumed here.

## First-customer measurement
Track verified organization, authorized contact, consent/source, qualification, proposed
scope, quoted price, approval, sandbox/live payment status, delivery acceptance and
actual costs. Count realized revenue and profit only from confirmed transactions and
incurred costs. No prospect, sale or market validation has been recorded in this cycle.
