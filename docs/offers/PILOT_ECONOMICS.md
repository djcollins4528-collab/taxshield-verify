# First-pilot unit economics — planning model

**Price selected: $1,950 USD, one-time. No payment or spending activated.**

Source inputs: PILOT_ECONOMICS.json. These are explicit planning assumptions, not
provider quotes, measured delivery times, actual processor pricing or market validation.
The former $1,500 hypothesis is superseded: with this baseline labor budget it would
leave only $252.20 (16.81%) before unallocated fixed overhead/tax, below our 25% floor.

At the baseline assumptions, the 25% margin price floor is **$1,671.75**.

Surplus includes an economic value for owner time; it is not net accounting profit.
It excludes unallocated business formation, legal, insurance, fixed software/hosting,
tax and acquisition overhead. Allocate those costs before declaring realized profit.
The 3.5% + $0.30 payment allowance is a budgeting assumption, not an asserted merchant rate.
Operations $75 and contingency $195 are per-engagement reserves, not verified bills.
No free-tier hosting is assumed approved for commercial operation.

## Commercial guardrails

- First pilot: one tenant/identity, five named relationships and one included retest.
- Recalculate with actual reviewer quote, permitted production infrastructure, actual
  merchant terms and allocated fixed overhead before issuing an executable quote.
- Require at least 25% modeled margin after valued owner labor and contingency. If
  costs exceed the threshold, reprice before signing or decline; do not omit review
  or silently reduce the promised work. $1,950 is selected, not market-proven.
- Moderate/severe overruns show the risk explicitly. Honor signed included work even
  if actual effort exceeds budget; no automatic overage. Narrow/reprice future work.
- The $975 kickoff payment does not authorize spending or eliminate refund exposure.
  Preserve liquidity for the selected refund policy; do not commit nonrefundable
  reviewer/tool costs against unearned refundable fees.
- No acquisition/ad spend, new paid subscriptions or hiring is authorized here.
- Track actual hours by stage, reviewer invoices, payment fees, variable costs,
  refunds and allocated overhead on the first pilot; replace estimates with evidence.
- A positive model is not evidence that a customer will pay this price. Validate
  willingness-to-pay through qualified discussions after operating scope is clear.

Refund stages reconcile to $1,950: collection $600, assessment/review $850, initial
report $300, retest/window $200. Two payments of $975 also reconcile to the same total.
One retest is included; the $200 is an allocation within the price, not an add-on.
