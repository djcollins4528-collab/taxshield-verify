Privacy & prelaunch contact

Status: This is an informational prelaunch website. There is no customer account, online form, checkout, analytics script or customer-document upload facility.

If you elect to contact the temporary advisory email address, your message will be handled by the receiving email provider under that provider's terms; ordinary email may not be appropriate for sensitive information. Do not send tax returns, Social Security numbers, passwords, taxpayer data, security logs or credentials.

Until the legal business identity and retention policies are finalized, no specialized customer data intake is offered. A complete operating privacy notice with a verified responsible legal entity, retention terms, rights requests and provider disclosures must replace this interim notice before production intake.

Site hosting may process basic request metadata, such as IP address and user agent, under the host's privacy documentation. No third-party analytics have been intentionally embedded.
