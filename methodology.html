DELIVERY STANDARD · VERSION 1.0 · DRAFT PRELAUNCH

A repeatable, defensible review—not a black-box score.

Every engagement uses the same control gates, evidence labels and quality checks. Scope remains explicit, and unverified does not mean failed.

Fixed engagement workflow

  1. Scope gate: Written customer authorization, named assets, approved methods, excluded activity, known subcontractors and agreed access boundaries.
  2. Evidence collection: Record timestamp, asset, method, source, raw/normalized observation, reviewer and limitations for each observation.
  3. Interpretation: Map each relevant observation to a documented assessment check. No implied compromise, regulatory violation or certainty beyond the evidence.
  4. Peer-style quality check: Reviewer confirms finding reproducibility, false-positive caveats, owner and feasible remedy before delivery.
  5. Delivery: Executive summary, scope, evidence register, ranked findings, action register, unresolved questions and walkthrough.
  6. Closure: Customer receives the report and a clear record of what was and was not assessed. Optional recheck is a separately scoped service.

External Snapshot — defined checks

Up to two customer-authorized email domains: SPF record presence and structural considerations; published DMARC policy; DKIM only for known selectors/senders provided or otherwise actually observable; mail configuration caveats. Up to two HTTPS hostnames: certificate identity and date validity, redirect behavior where observed, secure transport and a bounded set of response headers. This is not vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, or delivery of a comprehensive domain security rating.

Microsoft 365 Identity Review — defined checks

Includes External Snapshot and supported read-only observations about tenant identity and access posture, subject to consent, API permissions, licensing and data availability. Areas may include privileged role assignment, MFA/Conditional Access configuration evidence, tenant security signals and reviewable account controls. Missing visibility is explicitly marked—not inferred.

Quality gates before delivery

  • No target queried outside written scope; no credentials stored in assessment notes.
  • Every factual finding cites an evidence record and timestamp.
  • Each recommendation names an owner, a practical action and recheck criteria.
  • Severity has written rationale; non-applicable and unverified items are not scored as failures.
  • Report identifies methods, scope boundaries, uncertainties, and customer-action dependencies.
  • Files transferred only through an approved, access-controlled delivery channel once production processes are authorized.

Service limitations

The service does not certify compliance with IRS, FTC, GLBA or other requirements; does not conduct active exploitation; does not remediate systems; and cannot assure that a practice is free of threats. Prelaunch status: no customer access or purchase checkout is enabled.
