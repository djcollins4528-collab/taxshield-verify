Straight answers

Questions worth asking first.

Do we need a WISP even if we're a small tax firm?

The IRS says tax professionals need a Written Information Security Plan appropriate to the size and complexity of their practice. We do not determine your individual legal obligations. See the linked IRS guidance and consult appropriate advisors.

Does this make us compliant or certify our security?

No. This is a limited technical and operational assessment, not a regulatory audit, compliance opinion, penetration test, certification, or guarantee against incidents.

Do you need tax returns, client Social Security numbers, or passwords?

No. We do not request tax returns, taxpayer records, passwords, or confidential client financial information. Microsoft 365 work, if purchased, requires separately authorized and appropriately restricted read-only access.

What if we already have an IT provider?

That is often ideal. Our findings are intended to help you ask more focused questions and coordinate fixes with your existing provider. This service does not replace managed IT support.

What's the difference between a snapshot and a Microsoft 365 review?

The Snapshot is based on selected publicly observable technical settings and an owner questionnaire. The Microsoft 365 Review adds an explicitly authorized read-only examination of specified tenant settings. Some protections may remain unverified where permissions or telemetry are unavailable.

Are your assessments available to buy today?

Not yet. Packages and proposed prices are published for discussion and validation while commercial onboarding, agreements, and secure delivery controls are finalized. No payment is collected here.
