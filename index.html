THE PROBLEM

A security plan is useful.
Knowing your actual settings is better.

The IRS expects tax professionals to protect sensitive information and maintain a Written Information Security Plan. A document by itself cannot show whether a domain is configured correctly or an administrator’s access controls are effective. Our service provides a bounded configuration review, not a compliance certification.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

What you should know before booking.

Does the review make my firm IRS-compliant?

No. It is a limited technical configuration assessment, not a regulatory compliance certification or legal opinion. A WISP and broader security program still require appropriate ongoing management.

Do you need our client files or passwords?

No. We do not request taxpayer records, tax returns, mailbox content or passwords. Microsoft 365 reviews require explicit scoped authorization and supported read-only access.

Will you fix the issues for us?

No. The package covers assessment and recommendations only. Your IT provider or an authorized administrator implements changes; a later review can validate an agreed subset.

What if a control cannot be verified?

It is listed as not verified—not automatically marked failed or passed. The report explains why and what evidence could close the gap.

How fast is turnaround?

Timing is agreed in the statement of work after scope and access are confirmed. No guaranteed turnaround is offered before service operations are formally available.

Can we buy online today?

Not yet. This is a prelaunch information website. Payments and customer-system onboarding remain disabled until legal and operating prerequisites are completed.
