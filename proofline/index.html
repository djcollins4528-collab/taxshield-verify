Who we serve

Microsoft Entra Offboarding Evidence Review

The first pilot is designed for US architecture and engineering firms with 10–100 staff using Microsoft 365 Commercial. It covers one tenant and one recently departed employee with a cloud-only member account. Your administrator supplies the departure time, approves read-only access and names the relationships that matter.

What we check

We check whether sign-in is disabled, whether the records support a session-revocation action after departure, and whether up to five named group memberships or direct application assignments were removed.

What you receive

The planned package includes an independently reviewed assessment PDF, an evidence register and a remediation worksheet. In a 30-minute debrief, we walk through exceptions, evidence gaps and the next action for each finding.

What happens after a fix

Your administrator makes the changes. Request the included retest within ten business days of delivery. You receive an addendum with the new observations alongside the original findings.

The departure must be within seven calendar days before kickoff. The review covers the named directory controls at the recorded times. Mailbox permissions, devices, external SaaS and application-local sessions fall outside this pilot.

Our method

How a finding earns its conclusion

Agree on scope

At preflight we confirm authority, the account, named relationships and available records. We proceed only when the required sources are accessible.

Trace each check

We record what the source shows and when. A closed ticket alone cannot establish that access was removed.

Review the evidence

A separate reviewer checks the conclusion against its sources. Missing or ambiguous records can lead to an inconclusive finding.

Recheck changes

Each retest uses a new observation time and preserves the first result, so you can see what changed.
