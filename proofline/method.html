What the results mean

PASS requires attributable evidence for the exact agreed criterion. FAIL identifies an observed unmet criterion. INCONCLUSIVE identifies insufficient or ambiguous evidence; NOT_TESTED means the check was not executed. NOT_APPLICABLE requires a documented reason and reviewer concurrence, never missing evidence alone.

Overall “Scoped checks supported” requires all applicable mandatory checks to pass without unresolved contradictory evidence. “Exceptions found” means a failed control or unresolved contradictory evidence. “Insufficient evidence” means there is no observed failure but a mandatory check remains inconclusive or untested. None establishes universal access removal.

Current-state evidence, named relationship observations and available attributable directory audit records support the review. Retained sign-in events are optional context; no records is not proof of no access. Revocation evidence does not prove all application sessions have terminated. We do not sign in as the departed person or change the tenant.
